Privacy Controls

"Delete" should never be
one ambiguous button.

Your own view, the sender's live message, another participant's independent history, a group, a workspace, and an account are different domains. Norfold treats them differently.

Your side

What you control.

These actions affect your own view, your own devices, and your own relationship to content. They do not automatically affect another participant's independent copy.

Delete from my view

Remove a message from your normal view. The other participant's copy is unaffected.

YOU CONTROL

Private stars and pins

Mark conversations or messages for your own reference. Not visible to others.

YOU CONTROL

Message request policy

Control who can initiate contact with you. Strangers surface as requests, not direct messages.

YOU CONTROL

Block

Prevent a user from contacting you. Existing content in shared spaces is handled separately.

YOU CONTROL

Conversation notification preferences

Set per-conversation notification level: all messages, mentions only, or off.

YOU CONTROL

Archive retention

Manage your encrypted archive within your chosen provider's capabilities.

YOU CONTROL

Authorized devices

Review and revoke device authorizations on your account.

YOU CONTROL
Shared actions

Actions that affect both sides.

These actions request changes that affect more than your own view. They may be blocked, refused, or only partially enforceable depending on the other participant's state.

Delete for everyone

Requests removal from relay and blocks ordinary recovery paths. Cannot rewrite content already extracted on another device.

PARTIAL CONTROL

Disappearing message lifespan

Set a lifespan on messages. After expiry, relay and recovery paths are blocked. Independent device copies are not remotely wiped.

PARTIAL CONTROL

Whole DM deletion

Request deletion of an entire direct message thread. Requires both participants' agreement for full shared destruction.

PARTIAL CONTROL
Independent copies

What you do not control.

Deleting your account is not a magical remote wipe of content another participant legitimately possesses.

Another person's private archive

Content they have archived on their own provider is outside your control.

NOT YOUR CONTROL

Plaintext already extracted on their device

Once decrypted and stored locally on another device, you cannot remotely delete it.

NOT YOUR CONTROL

A shared DM copy when they refuse shared destruction

Full shared deletion requires both parties. If the other participant does not agree, their copy remains.

NOT YOUR CONTROL

Submitted report evidence after consent

Once you have explicitly submitted a report, the evidence package is in the moderation system.

NOT YOUR CONTROL
Pre-release caveat

Privacy controls are implemented and under validation. Some controls described here may not yet be fully operational. Behavior may change before public release.
