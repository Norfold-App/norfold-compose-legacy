What Norfold can see

Operational data.

Running a service requires some operational visibility. This is what Norfold has access to by design.

Account identity

Username, email address, and account creation metadata.

OPERATIONAL

Workspace membership

Which workspaces and groups you belong to, and your role within them.

OPERATIONAL

Routing metadata

Message delivery signals, push event routing, and membership coordination. Not message content.

OPERATIONAL

Managed workspace content

In Managed-mode workspaces, content is not end-to-end encrypted and may be accessible to workspace operators.

MODE-DEPENDENT

Abuse reports

Evidence submitted explicitly by a reporter. Not collected silently from private conversations.

CONSENT-ONLY
What Norfold should not see

Private content.

These are architectural goals. Pre-release validation is ongoing — these are design intentions, not certified guarantees.

E2EE message content

Personal DM and personal group message content is encrypted before leaving your device. The server should not be able to read it.

BY DESIGN

Private archive contents

Your long-term message history lives in your chosen archive provider (e.g. Google Drive), not on Norfold's servers.

BY DESIGN

Local-only workspace data

Data you have not explicitly synced or shared stays on your device.

BY DESIGN

Your cryptographic keys

Private keys are generated and stored on your devices. Norfold does not hold them.

BY DESIGN
Providers

Third-party infrastructure.

Norfold uses third-party providers for hosting, storage, and delivery. Provider independence is an architectural goal — the design should not trap the product in a single provider.

⬡

Hosting and database

Backend infrastructure runs on cloud providers. Operational data is subject to those providers' terms and applicable law.

◫

Archive provider

Google Drive is the first supported archive provider. Your archive data is subject to Google's terms. A self-hosted option is planned.

IMPLEMENTED
Deletion

What deletion actually means.

Deletion in a distributed system is not a single button. Different domains have different rules.

Delete from my view

Removes content from your local view. Does not affect another participant's independent copy.

YOU CONTROL

Delete for everyone

Requests removal from relay and blocks ordinary recovery paths. Cannot rewrite content already extracted on another device.

PARTIAL CONTROL

Account deletion

Removes your account and associated server-side data. Does not delete content another participant legitimately possesses.

SCOPED

Another person's copy

You do not control content another participant has independently received and stored.

NOT YOUR CONTROL
