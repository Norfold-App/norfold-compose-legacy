Account Deletion

What deletion actually means.

Account deletion removes your account and associated server-side data. It does not delete content another participant legitimately possesses on their own device.

What happens

When you delete your account.

Account identity removed

Your username, profile, and account metadata are removed from Norfold's servers.

HAPPENS

Server-side data removed

Data associated with your account on Norfold's infrastructure is deleted according to the deletion policy.

HAPPENS

Relay data purged

Any remaining relay data associated with your account is cleared.

HAPPENS

Workspace membership ended

You are removed from workspaces and groups you belonged to.

HAPPENS

Another person's independent copy

Content another participant has independently received and stored on their device is not affected by your account deletion.

NOT AFFECTED

Your local device data

Data on your own devices is not automatically wiped by account deletion. You manage your own devices.

NOT AUTOMATIC
Grace period

Before deletion is final.

Account deletion includes a grace period during which you can cancel the deletion if you change your mind.

◎

Grace window

After initiating deletion, a grace period allows you to cancel before the deletion becomes permanent. Signing back in does not automatically cancel a pending deletion.

IMPLEMENTED
◫

Archive choice

Before deletion completes, you can choose what happens to your encrypted archive. Your archive provider holds your data independently of your Norfold account.

IMPLEMENTED
Ownership

Ownership requirements.

Some deletion paths require ownership transfer before the account can be deleted.

Workspace ownership

If you are the sole owner of a workspace, you must transfer ownership or delete the workspace before deleting your account.

REQUIRED FIRST

Group ownership

Personal groups you own may need to be transferred or dissolved before account deletion.

REQUIRED FIRST
Independent copies

What you cannot delete remotely.

Deleting your account is not a magical remote wipe of content another participant legitimately possesses.

⊘

Two independent sides

In a conversation between two people, each participant has their own independent copy of the exchange. Your account deletion removes your side from Norfold's servers. It does not reach into another person's device or archive and delete their independent copy. This is a fundamental property of distributed systems — not a policy choice that can be overridden.

BY DESIGN
Pre-release caveat

Account deletion procedures are implemented and under validation. Some steps described here may not yet be fully operational during the pre-release period.
