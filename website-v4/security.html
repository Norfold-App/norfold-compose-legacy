Security

Security you can inspect,
not a vibe.

Rather than one giant "secure" label, Norfold separates personal encryption, workspace governance, temporary delivery, private archives, deletion, and support boundaries.

Security modes

Two modes. Zero silent downgrades.

Personal DMs and groups are designed around end-to-end encryption. Workspaces may use E2EE or Managed mode — and members should see which mode they are in.

PERSONAL CHAT

Always E2EE

DMs and personal groups are designed around end-to-end encryption. The server moves what it does not need to understand. No silent downgrade to a weaker mode.

END-TO-END ENCRYPTED
WORKSPACE

Owner-selected. Disclosed.

Workspace owners choose E2EE or Managed mode. Members should be able to see which mode they are in before participating. The choice is explicit, not hidden.

E2EE MANAGED
⊘
No silent downgrade. The security mode you see is the mode you are in.
Device identity

Your account survives a lost cryptographic identity.

Same username, profile, contacts, groups, workspaces, roles, and ownership. New cryptographic identity, new security code, and a quiet security-change event — no reinvite chain.

@

Losing a device or resetting cryptographic identity does not destroy your account. Social graph, workspace membership, and ownership survive the change.

Relay

Relay, not permanent private history.

For E2EE chat, Norfold's backend is designed to move encrypted content, coordinate delivery, then purge temporary relay data according to policy.

What the relay does

1
Move ciphertextTemporary delivery of encrypted envelopes to authorized recipients.
2
Coordinate membershipRouting metadata, push events, and operational signals.
3
Purge after deliveryRelay data cleared according to policy — not kept as a permanent archive.

What the relay does not do

✕
Read message contentE2EE envelopes are opaque to the server.
✕
Keep a permanent archiveLong-term history lives on your devices and your chosen archive provider.
✕
Manufacture lost historyIf relay data is purged and your archive is gone, it cannot be reconstructed.
Support

No support backdoor.

If every trusted device, archive, and recovery method is gone, Norfold cannot manufacture the missing private history. Support cannot silently bypass E2EE.

✓

Support can

Explain supported recovery paths. Help with account procedures. Help with ownership. Process explicit reports.

DEFINED
⊘

Support cannot

Manufacture lost encrypted history. Silently bypass E2EE. Request your master key as normal procedure. Secretly browse private chats.

Reporting

Reporting by consent.

E2EE evidence is packaged locally only when the reporter deliberately submits it. Reports are not silently collected from private conversations.

⚑

Reporter-initiated only

When a user chooses to report content, the evidence package is assembled on their device and submitted explicitly. The server does not have standing access to E2EE message content for moderation purposes.
