Data & Recovery

Messages move.
Ownership stays clear.

A secure messaging system is more than an encryption label. Norfold separates the live cryptographic session, temporary relay, local history, private archive, and destructive-state rules.

Lifecycle

From creation to deletion.

Each stage of a message's life is a distinct system with distinct rules. They are not the same thing.

Five stages

Create.
Relay.
Keep.
Archive.
Remove.

Understanding which stage you are in determines what is possible — and what is not.

01 / CREATE

Your device creates the content.

Private message content is encrypted on an authorized device before normal E2EE relay. Local-first UI does not wait for a round-trip merely to display what you just sent. The plaintext never leaves your device unencrypted.

02 / RELAY

The server moves what it does not need to understand.

Temporary ciphertext delivery, membership routing, push events, and operational metadata. The relay is not a permanent readable private message archive. Relay data is purged according to policy after delivery.

03 / KEEP

Your devices hold long-term history.

The authoritative copy of your message history lives on your authorized devices. This is the primary long-term store — not the relay, not the server.

04 / ARCHIVE

Optional encrypted backup to your chosen provider.

Google Drive is the first supported archive provider. Your archive data is encrypted before upload. The archive provider is a replaceable implementation — a future self-hosted option should not require redesigning the privacy model.

05 / REMOVE

Deletion and lifespan stop ordinary resurrection.

Delete-for-everyone and explicitly destructive lifespans block normal relay, recovery-relay, and routine archive restoration paths after destruction. They do not rewrite content another participant has independently received and stored on their own device.

Recovery

What recovery can and cannot do.

Recovery is bounded by what was preserved. It cannot manufacture history that was never archived or was explicitly destroyed.

Restore from archive

If you have an encrypted archive on your provider, authorized devices can restore from it.

SUPPORTED

Recover after device loss

Account continuity survives device loss. New cryptographic identity, same account. History requires archive access.

SUPPORTED

Recover relay-purged messages

Once relay data is purged, it cannot be recovered from the server. Archive is the only path.

NOT POSSIBLE

Recover explicitly deleted content

Content deleted with destructive intent is not recoverable through normal paths by design.

NOT POSSIBLE

Recover without any archive

If no archive exists and relay data is purged, the history is gone. Norfold cannot manufacture it.

NOT POSSIBLE
Pre-release caveat

Recovery paths are implemented and under validation. Behavior may change before public release. Do not rely on recovery guarantees during the pre-release period.
