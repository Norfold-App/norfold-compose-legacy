# Norfold motion specification

## Identity invariant
The final mark is the exact traced `brand/norfold-glyph.svg` geometry. Motion may reveal,
fold, illuminate, or settle the mark but must not replace its silhouette with a different N.

## Canonical colors
- Ink: `#1E2024`
- Dark-mode ink: `#F6F7FB`
- Dot: `#56AA5F`
- Accent/control green: `#3D7C45`
- Soft green: `#A7D8AB`

## Choreography
1. Left structural stroke reveals.
2. Diagonal fold follows with green-to-ink energy.
3. Right structural stroke settles.
4. Green dot arrives with small overshoot and ripple.
5. Exact final glyph becomes the only persistent form.
6. Idle animation is extremely subtle: occasional sheen + dot breathing.

The website SVG uses curved reveal paths instead of the older three straight reveal lines.
The app can port the same choreography without changing the final brand geometry.
