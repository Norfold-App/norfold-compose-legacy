Brand

The Norfold identity.

Canonical mark, palette, motion rules, and usage guidelines. The final glyph may not be redrawn. The animation may evolve. The green dot is not decorative.

Mark

Canonical glyph.

The Norfold mark is a traced glyph. Do not redraw, recolor, or deform it. The final silhouette must always remain canonical.

Motion

When the mark moves.

Motion is allowed only where it adds meaning. The final glyph silhouette must always be the canonical form. Continuous purposeless animation is not allowed.

Navigation

Use the static canonical mark. No animation in the nav.

Palette

Canonical colors.

Green is a signal, not a background flood. Most surfaces remain neutral. Large green gradients are prohibited. Purple and coral startup gradients are prohibited.

Rules

Do not.

Recolor the canonical green dot

The green dot is #56AA5F. It is not decorative and must not be changed arbitrarily.

PROHIBITED

Deform the final glyph

The traced glyph silhouette is fixed. Do not stretch, skew, or redraw it.

PROHIBITED

Use coral or purple gradients

These are V2-era identity drift. They are not the Norfold identity.

PROHIBITED

Flood large surfaces with green

Green is a signal color. It should not dominate backgrounds or large fills.

PROHIBITED

Animate continuously without purpose

Motion must communicate meaning. Idle looping animation without purpose is not allowed.
