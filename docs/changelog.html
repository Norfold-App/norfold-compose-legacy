Changelog

0.1.0-alpha.3 · Alpha · August 2026

Editors

Docs, Flow and Spreadsheet became one editor over a shared document model, so a table, chart, image or diagram behaves the same wherever you meet it. Chart Studio, Image Focus, Shape Builder, the equation builder and the diagram builder arrived with it, along with slash commands, named styles, bundled offline fonts, and a history and recovery browser held on the device.

Tasks

Table, Board, Feed, Calendar, Chart, List, Timeline and Matrix views with manual order preserved, and a management surface for task properties. Task reminders now fire — earlier builds stored them and never delivered them.

Chat

Encrypted messages, media and archives; personal groups, workspace channels and message requests; disappearing messages, privacy-safe abuse reporting, and per-conversation mute.

Notifications

A Notifications section in Settings covers task reminders and quiet hours. It also keeps the in-app and email preferences onboarding asked about, but this build has no email sender and no in-app tips surface, so neither choice changes anything yet. Quiet hours hold a reminder until the window ends rather than dropping it, and a message somebody sent always arrives — the most any preference does is take away its sound.

Accessibility

Every focusable control the keyboard can reach shows a focus ring. Screen readers announce a setting's name, role and state as one thing, long-press actions are discoverable, secondary text meets AA contrast on both canvases, and directional icons mirror in right-to-left layouts.

Testing notice

Norfold remains alpha software. This build is not release-signed and has not been certified on a physical device. Keep independent copies of important information, export a backup before testing import, export or sync, and report reproducible issues through the private channel that supplied the build.
