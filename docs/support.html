Support cannot

What support is unable to do — by design.

These are not policy choices that could be overridden with enough escalation. They are architectural constraints.

Manufacture lost encrypted history

If relay data is purged and no archive exists, the history is gone. It cannot be reconstructed.

✕ NOT POSSIBLE

Silently bypass E2EE

E2EE message content is encrypted before it reaches the server. Support cannot read it.

✕ NOT POSSIBLE

Request your master key as normal procedure

Norfold does not hold your private keys. A support request for your master key is not a legitimate procedure.

✕ NOT LEGITIMATE

Secretly browse private chats

E2EE personal conversations are opaque to the server. Support has no standing access to their content.

✕ NOT POSSIBLE

Delete another person's independent copy

Content another participant has independently received and stored on their device is outside Norfold's control.

✕ NOT POSSIBLE

Guarantee recovery without an archive

Recovery requires an archive. Without one, there is nothing to restore from.

✕ NOT POSSIBLE
